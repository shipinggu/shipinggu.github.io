A systematic research framework系统性研究体系

This series of patent applications addresses an integrated optical fiber chemistry framework rather than isolated structural modifications. Its central concept is the programmable coupling of light, chemical reactions and intelligent control.本系列专利并非针对某一局部结构的小改小革，而是围绕“光纤化学”这一完整技术体系展开，其核心是光场、化学反应与智能控制的可编程耦合。

Optical fiber光纤 → Programmable light可编程光场 → Reaction interface反应界面 → AI controlAI控制

Seven systematic innovations光纤化学：“光纤化学”体系的系统性创新点

01

New principle新原理

Optical fibers serve as a platform for controlling optical fields within chemical reactors and at reaction interfaces, enabling light to act as a programmable resource in catalysis.将光纤作为化学反应器内及界面光场调控的基础平台，使光场作为“可编程资源”参与催化过程。

02

New theoretical framework新理论框架

An integrated design framework couples multilayer interfaces, multiple physical fields, optical intensity, phase and spectral control in fiber networks with electrode processes and mass transport.多层界面、多场耦合、光纤网络的光强、相位及频段调控，与电极过程和传质过程耦合的整体设计思想。

03

New chemical platforms新化学平台

Fiber-based reaction platforms for fuel cells, ammonia synthesis, CO₂ reduction, and methanol synthesis and utilization.基于光纤的燃料电池、合成氨、CO₂还原、甲醇合成与利用等平台化反应系统。

04

New methods新方法

Integrated programmable control of wavelength, optical intensity, light direction, voltage, current, current density, temperature, reactant feed, pH and catalyst type (thermal, electrochemical and photocatalytic).可编程波长、光强、光线指向、电压、电流、电流密度、温度、反应物进给、pH 调控以及催化剂类型（热催化、电催化、光催化）的综合可编程控制。

05

New devices and structures新装置与结构

Optical fiber membrane electrodes, sandwich-structured fiber membrane electrodes, multilayer-interface fiber reactors and two-dimensional fiber networks for optical intensity control.光纤化学膜电极、三明治结构光纤膜电极、多层界面光纤反应器、光纤二维网络光强调控平台等。

06

New applications新应用场景

Optical fiber chemical fuel cells, ammonia synthesis, methanol production and utilization, and analytical instrumentation workstations.光纤化学燃料电池、光纤化学合成氨、光纤化学甲醇制备与利用、光纤化学仪器分析工作站等。

07

Programmable chemistry and AI laboratories / factories可编程化学与AI实验室／工厂

Optical fiber chemical reactors form the hardware core of automated, programmable chemical laboratories and factories integrated with AI and intelligent control.以光纤化学反应堆为硬件核心，结合AI与智能控制，实现自动化、可编程的化学实验室及工厂。
